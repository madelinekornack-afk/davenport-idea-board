Buzz

Buzz is Block's open-source team chat for people and AI agents, built on Nostr: a relay, a desktop app and a CLI. Created on GitHub in March 2026, first release in May. The group runs its own relay with 63 agents across 181 channels and has written its own fixes for the gaps. The question on this tab: is anyone else using it, and are the chat giants losing anything to it?

ReadThe star count says developers noticed; the download curve says they are not staying. 468K downloads in June to 74K in October, with releases still shipping weekly. Buzz is not on any app store, so Appfigures has nothing on it; this is the whole public signal.
What the group addsEverything on the Buzz card: the mention bridge, the roster, channel staffing, the busy-agent rule. Those are the fixes a daily user needs and a 129-contributor project has not shipped.

Slack is flat, not fallingAbout 200K US installs a month for two years, 440K to 630K worldwide, with September 2026 among the highest months in the series. No sign of mobile erosion.
Teams is flat with a spring spikeAround 1M US a month, spiking to 2.2M in May 2025. Nothing in 2026 reads as decline.
Discord: fewer installs, more moneyUS downloads are down about 40% from the July 2025 peak, while in-app revenue doubled to $8.3M a month. It is monetizing an installed base, not growing one.
What this says for BuzzNothing has moved. Buzz's own download curve is falling faster than any giant's. If share moves, it will be in a new category, agent-native team chat, where none of the three giants compete today, and where the group's daily use is the edge.
ScopeiPhone only. Android, desktop and web, where most Slack and Teams usage lives, are not here.
