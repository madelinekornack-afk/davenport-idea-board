Clark Market Scan

Nothing on the App Store is Clark. Every piece of it ships as a separate app, so this is what each piece earns on iPhone in the US right now. Bars are modeled net revenue for the last 30 days; download counts sit next to the name where Appfigures models them. Each chart scales to its own leader, so compare within a group, and use the group totals to compare across.

What it says for Clark

Transactions is the only Clark piece with real consumer moneyFive budgeting apps clear $350K a month on US iPhone alone, led by Rocket Money at $1.2M and YNAB, Copilot and Monarch at $580K to $870K each. All four charge $100 to 200 a year. That is roughly $45M a year of US iPhone revenue for the top five, before web and Android.
Nobody pays for the data layer by itselfThe lifelog and own-your-data group is empty: Limitless, Exist, Gyroscope and Saner have no US estimate at all. Steve's "it's infrastructure" point shows up directly. The money appears only when there is a GUI on top (budgeting) or an assistant on top (ChatGPT, Claude).
The assistant-on-top pool is two orders of magnitude biggerChatGPT $68M, Claude $28M, Grok $13M in 30 days. Muse from Meta had 2.7M US downloads in the window for $0.8M, so it is being given away for now. Pocket, a paid "AI personal assistant," made $924K on 33K downloads, about $28 a download, which says a narrow, expensive assistant can sell.
Email GUIs do not monetize on iPhoneGmail, Yahoo and Outlook own the category and are free. Spark makes $127K; Superhuman, Shortwave and Canary do not register. Phil's Mac and iOS email client is a retention feature, not the business.
Receipt matching is a business featureQuickBooks makes $2.1M; the consumer receipt apps make $20K to $85K each. The receipt-to-charge step Clark already does is worth something inside a transactions product, not as its own app.
Subscription audit is a feature that sells alone, badlyMonoBoost and Subpilot make $150K and $112K a month on weekly subscriptions. Rocket Money built the same thing into a budgeting app and is the category leader. Same lesson as receipts.

Caveats

ScopeUS, iPhone, App Store only. Android, web billing (Superhuman, Shortwave, Lunch Money, YNAB's site) and desktop are not in these numbers, so email and power-user tools are understated.
EstimatesAppfigures models from ranks and ratings. Small apps fall below its threshold and show as "not modeled," which means small, not zero. A blank download count means the download model did not resolve, not that nobody installed it.
MethodKeyword Inspector searches for each app's name plus the category terms budget app, expense tracker, email app, receipt scanner, ai assistant, personal assistant and subscription tracker. Only the top 10 results per search carry estimates. No tracking slots or API credits were used. Raw data in ~/Projects/appfigures-clark.
