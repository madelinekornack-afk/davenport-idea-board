Form-check video coach

Film a set, a run or a swing on your phone and get told what you are doing wrong, how to fix it, and whether you are getting better. The model watches the video the way a coach would. The proven version of this is golf swing analysis; the open version is everything else.

Maddy, Nov 8: "I would 1000% use this as an app. Like taking a video and showing how I'm fucking it up, how I could improve, track improvement over time."

How it fits together

Everything it would do

ProposedRecord or upload a setA phone propped on a bench or a friend filming. No sensor, no wearable, no special angle.
ProposedPose estimationJoint angles, bar path, depth, tempo, from a single camera. Commodity now; every golf app does it.
ProposedCritique in plain languageA video model watches the clip and says what went wrong the way a coach would, not as a skeleton overlay.
ProposedOne cue per sessionMaddy's ask: "showing how I'm fucking it up, how I could improve." One fix, not a dashboard.
ProposedCompare to last timeThe same lift three weeks ago, side by side, so improvement is visible rather than claimed.
ProposedProgress over timeA score per movement that moves. "Track improvement over time" was the third thing Maddy named.
ProposedAny movement, not one sportGolf has a dozen swing analyzers. Lifting, running and everyday movement have none that make money. That is the gap and the risk.
NextShare with a coachA human reviews the flagged clips. The golf and coaching apps (Onform, CoachNow) monetize exactly this.
NextFeeds the health pictureForm scores next to sleep, weight and labs. Could be a feature of the health copilot rather than its own app.

Where the group is on it

Why it is rank 8Strong pull from the target user, and it sits in the same health vein as the copilot. Video models can now critique movement well enough to be useful. Market 3, Edge 3, Evidence 3, Build 3.
The honest read from the numbersGeneral gym form-check is a graveyard: seven AI form apps launched since 2023 and none has a hundred ratings. The only form analysis that makes money is golf, where there is one movement, a paying audience and a filming culture. The wedge is one movement with a community, not "AI form check."
EdgeCould be a feature of the health copilot rather than its own app. Max's audience skews toward people who track things. No inside expertise here the way Orca and Media Studio have it.
BuildPure software, self-serve, no sales cycle: squarely inside Phil's one-month rule. The hard part is design and product thinking about which movement and which cue, not the model.
Where it standsOne text from Maddy with a tweet. Steve and Phil have not replied. Whiteboard question for tonight: which single movement first, and does it live inside the health copilot?

What each piece earns today

Appfigures estimates, US, iPhone, last 30 days (Oct 12 to Nov 10, 2026), net revenue. Each chart is one thing the coach would do and the apps that sell that one thing today.

Golf proves the model and warns about the restAbout $340K a month across nine golf swing apps, with the biggest, Swing Coach, launched only in March 2025. Same technology pointed at the gym: zero.
Lifters pay for tracking, not for formFitbod $666K and Strong $216K a month. The money in the gym is the log. A form coach that is also the log has a business; a form coach alone has seven dead competitors.
Human coaches are the monetizing layerOnform and CoachNow make $44K and $13K selling video review to coaches. Outside golf, people pay a person to look at the clip, not a model.
