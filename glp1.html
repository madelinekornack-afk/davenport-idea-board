Two winners, $565K a month between themMeAgain and Shotsy make about $6.8M a year on US iPhone combined. That is the whole category's in-app revenue. Both buy Apple Ads on "glp-1 tracker" and "ozempic," and so does MyFitnessPal, which now ranks for "glp1" organically: the incumbent is moving in.
Twenty-five trackers launched in 2026Up from nine in 2025. Four cleared the download model and one makes money. The shot-log-plus-reminder app is a solved problem with a two-year head start, and the long tail proves it.
The one new thing that worked is peptides, brieflyPeptidePal and Peptide Tracker Log both launched in spring 2026 and both spiked to 800 to 1,700 ratings a month in June and July, then fell by 80%. There is demand for peptide dosing that the GLP-1 apps do not serve, and nobody has held it.

What this says for idea 8

The card was right: another shot log is dead on arrivalTwenty-five of them shipped this year and one makes money. Steve's "would likely crush" has to mean the layer the trackers lack: the regimen compared against bloodwork, food and labs in one place, which MeAgain is already inching toward with an AI coach.
The market is real but small on the phoneAbout $7M a year in the US App Store for the top two, with MeAgain still growing at 4,500 ratings a month. The money in GLP-1 is in prescribing (Mochi Health went from 229 to 2,000 ratings a month in a year) and in the drugs, not the log.
Where the audience helpsGLP-1 users skew to the income band Maxinomics has, and the peptide spike says the Bryan Johnson crowd is in there too. That is a distribution edge for a Rythm-style partner (idea 9) as much as for a tracker.
ScopeUS, iPhone, App Store only. Android, web sign-ups and web billing are not here, and most of these companies sell on the web, so revenue understates them.

ReadThis is the smallest pool on the board. Fifty percent of the entire US iPhone tracker category is a $4M business, and the leader already has it. The only band that pays here is the audience one, and only if the product is the food-plus-labs layer the card describes rather than another log: 1% of the audience at $50 is more than Shotsy makes today.
How to read the bandsCreator-sold products convert 1% to 3% of an engaged audience, so the 1% row is the realistic case, 10% is a multi-year ceiling, and 50% is the size of the whole audience spending, which no product reaches.
