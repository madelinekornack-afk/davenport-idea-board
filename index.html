Nov 11 session: the engine, as builtTally takes Plaid webhooks and runs two stages. First, merchant cleaning with a fast model, a web-search fallback when confidence is low (account numbers and names stripped before the search), aliases saved as "machine cleaned" with no review queue, Amazon direct told apart from Amazon Marketplace. Then one of 47 economic types (merchant purchase, someone sending you money, a card payoff, a transfer, brokerage in or out), tuned on 450 of Phil's own hand-labelled transactions to 94 to 95%. Clark ingests every email, hands receipt candidates to Tally to pin to the transaction, and searches by vectors plus keywords: "Morning Brew contract" found a 2023 thread with Devin Emery that never used the word. Local files are next, a small embedding model on the Mac, because Spotlight cannot see inside a PDF. Node and Python on AWS, about $350 a month for Phil alone, and every customer gets their own CloudFormation stack so no two people share a table.
Who pays, per PhilNot a $200 consumer tool. High-net-worth operators and executives with several entities, real estate and deal flow, who lose contracts in email and dread tax season: the people who already answer his videos (Craig Fuller of FreightWaves, Austin Rief of Morning Brew, Steve's friends Graham and Gnome, "total mess").
